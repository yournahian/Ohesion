Ohesion — 100% UI-Driven Community Engine

Forget memorizing 30 different slash commands and broken parameter forms. Ohesion is designed from the ground up to be operated entirely through native Discord Buttons, Dropdown Menus, and Pop-up Modals.

🖱️
How Members & Admins Use Ohesion:
• Members never type commands: They visit #cohesion-hub and click interactive buttons for their Profile, Daily Points, Role Shop, Support Tickets, and Quests.
• Admins manage with visual controls: Every setting—from turning modules on/off, whitelisting links per channel, to exporting CSV files—is done via visual buttons and Discord modal popups!
🔘
1-Click Buttons
Instant actions for claiming daily points, entering raffles, placing auction bids, and closing support tickets.
📝
Discord Modals
Clean, error-free popup forms for ticket issues, quest submissions, raffle creations, and date filters.
🎛️
Dropdown Selectors
Multi-select menus for enabling/disabling features and selecting channels for custom link whitelists.

🚀 1-Click Setup & Automated Channels

To initialize Ohesion, an administrator runs `/setup` just once. The bot automatically creates a dedicated ecosystem in your server:

📁 COHESION HQ (Category Automatically Created)
Everything your community needs is cleanly organized into 4 dedicated channels:
  • #cohesion-hub — The permanent interactive Member Dashboard with all action buttons.
  • #cohesion-quests — Visual cards for social media raids, Twitter quests, and website visits.
  • #cohesion-levels — Dedicated level-up announcements channel (keeps #general chat clean!).
  • #cohesion-logs — Private security audit logs, quest verifications, and marketplace purchases.

🧭 Member Experience: The Interactive Hub

In `#cohesion-hub`, members have an always-active, self-updating visual control center. They never have to guess what commands exist:

📌 Live Buttons in #cohesion-hub
Members simply click these buttons to perform any action:
🎁 Claim Daily 👤 My Profile 🏆 Leaderboard 🛍️ Shop / Market 🎫 Support Ticket 🐦 Connect Twitter ⚔️ Chaos Clash
Button Clicked What Opens / Happens
`🎁 Claim Daily` Instantly awards daily points with consecutive day streak bonus multipliers.
`👤 My Profile` Opens an ephemeral private card showing Level, XP progress bar, Points (CP), linked Twitter, and wallet.
`🏆 Leaderboard` Shows the top 10 community leaderboard ranked by Points, XP, and Quests completed.
`🛍️ Shop / Market` Opens an interactive dropdown menu to purchase Discord roles and server perks with points.

`⚔️ Chaos Clash` Opens the live battle arena lobby to join the match or wager points on fighting champions.

🐦 Twitter / X Raid Card

  1. Member clicks the tweet link directly from the card.

🎛️ Server Modes & Module Toggles

Turn features on or off in real-time with visual dropdown menus:

💡
Turning Off Points & Level-Up Spam:
• Option A (1-Click): Select `🟣 Social & Roles Only`. Points and level-up messages turn off instantly while Quests and Support Tickets stay active!
• Option B (Custom): In `Custom Modular Mode`, click `[ ⚙️ Select Active Modules ]` and uncheck `Points Economy (CP)` and `Leveling & XP`!

Active modules you can toggle with checkboxes: Points Economy, Leveling & XP, Raffles & Giveaways, Live Auctions, Marketplace & Roles, Quests & Social Feeds, Referral Codes, Attendance & VC XP, Trivia & Quizzes, Battle Engine, and Support Tickets.

🛡️ Cohesion Shield & Per-Channel Link Whitelists

Clicking 🛡️ AutoMod & Shield in the admin panel gives you complete security controls:

🔗 Custom Channel Links Manager
Admins click 🔗 Custom Channel Links to set independent link rules for every channel:
  1. Pick any channel from the native Discord Channel Select Menu.
  2. Click 🛡️ Set Allowed Links ➔ A modal opens where you type approved domains (e.g. x.com, twitter.com, rialo.io).
  3. In that channel: approved links pass cleanly; unapproved links are auto-deleted with a friendly reminder!
  4. Or click Allow All (for media channels) or Block All (for strict general chat).

✈️ Telegram Cross-Platform Handshake

Connect your Discord server to an official Telegram group in 3 easy steps:

  1. Add @OhesionBot to your Telegram group as an administrator.
  2. In your Telegram group, send /link. The bot generates a 6-digit handshake code.
  3. In Discord, run /pair <code>. Both platforms are now bridged and cross-synced!
